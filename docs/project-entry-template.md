# Project entry template

Use this to turn a conversation or rough notes into a consistent portfolio entry.
The owner does not need to fill it in. Copy only material intended to be public.
This is a writing template, not a build input; Projects pages are not implemented yet.

## Capture record

- **Working title:**
- **Stable slug:**
- **Capture issue:**
- **When:** exact date, approximate period, or unknown
- **Status:** idea / experiment / prototype / in use / completed / archived
- **Publication stage:** captured / checked / drafted / reviewed / published
- **Owner's role:** what Sharron personally did; credit collaborators separately
- **Capabilities demonstrated:** up to three, each supported by the account below
- **Public evidence:** repository, demo, approved screenshot or other shareable source
- **Still to confirm:** missing facts, approximate dates, evidence or wording
- **Published URL / PR / commit:** add after release

## Public draft

### One sentence

What is it, who is it for and why might someone want to look?

### What needed to change?

Describe the concrete problem and relevant constraints in plain English.

### What I did

Explain the decisions, contribution and approach. Name tools only when they help
explain the work. Distinguish individual work from team work and AI assistance.

### What changed?

Use a specific observed result. Support figures with an identifiable public source
or record that the owner must verify them before publication. Where outcomes are
unknown, state what was built or learned without implying measured impact.

### What I learned / what comes next

Optional. State limitations and next steps honestly, especially for prototypes.

### Have a look

Optional links, approved images and short captions. A historical project can stand
on a clear account even when there is no public demo. Never invent a testimonial,
a result, personal experience or a link to fill a gap.

## Ready for review

- [ ] Owner's contribution, status and dates are accurate or explicitly approximate.
- [ ] Claims and figures have been checked; unsupported claims are removed.
- [ ] Names, images and linked evidence are suitable for public sharing.
- [ ] The entry sounds like the owner and makes sense without specialist knowledge.
- [ ] The owner has reviewed the wording before it is merged for publication.
